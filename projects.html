My Projects

Here I'll share a description of each of my projects and the intention behind them.

Project 1: Blog

Description: A blog that follows my journey into the world of tech.

Intention: As my 1st "official" project, I wanted to build something I could continue developing over time. I wanted my first project to be something personal and professional that I could share with everyone. Miranda's blog 🌸 is my personal brand and archive where I document my growth, showcase my consistency and express my creativity as I transition into tech.

Project 2: Progress Tracker

Description: A customizable tracker that helps organize daily, weekly and monthly habits and tasks.

Intention: Tracking habits is a very important part of self-improvement, and I wanted to create a system that works for my personal needs. With this project I'll learn to structure and design interactive features. I can also continue improving this project over time.

Project 3: Linktree

Description: A linktree that acts as a home for all my socials and important links for networking.

Intention: This project started with a color palette that inspired me. I wanted to see if I could make something that felt like the sun. I think I did! Building it also deepened my understanding of flexbox and how it works across nested containers.
