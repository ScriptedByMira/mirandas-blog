Day 26

Today I completed a FreeCodeCamp lab building a pricing plans layout page. The trickiest part was figuring out how to add ✓ and ✗ symbols to list items without touching the HTML. I knew I needed a pseudo class but wasn’t sure which one. Well, turns out CSS, ::before pseudo-elements will work to "paint" content right before any element. I also got a much better feel for how flexbox works at two levels: the container arranging the cards, and each card arranging its own content independently.
